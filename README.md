1 Organisation Overview:
•	Name: Installations By Tsakani (Hair Salon)

•	Brief History:  Installations by Tsakani is a small business that operates as a hair salon. It was founded on 1 April 2026 by Tsakani Mthethwa, who is also the owner of the business. The salon was established with the aim of providing quality and affordable hair care services to the community. Since its establishment, Installations by Tsakani has focused on customer satisfaction, professional service, and creating a welcoming environment for its clients. As a small business, it also contributes to local economic growth by serving the community and promoting entrepreneurship.

•	Employer: Tsakani (Owner and Manager)
Number of Employees: 1
Installations By Tsakani is a small business operated by one person, Tsakani, who is both the employer and employee. She manages the daily operations of the salon, provides hair services, handles customer enquiries and appointments and manages the overall business.

•	Mission Statement: At Installations By Tsakani, our mission is to enhance every client’s beauty and confidence by providing high-quality hair installation and styling services in a welcoming, professional, and caring environment. We are committed to delivering exceptional customer service, using quality products, staying up to date with the latest hair trends, and ensuring every client leaves feeling beautiful and valued.
•	Vision Statement: Our vision is to become one of the most trusted and preferred hair salons in South Africa, known for excellence, creativity, and customer satisfaction. We aim to inspire confidence through beautiful hairstyles while building a brand that empowers women, creates employment opportunities, and sets the standard for quality hair care and installations.

•	Target Audience: Women aged 18–50 who value quality hair care and stylish hair installations. Students, young professionals, working women, and entrepreneurs looking for affordable, fashionable hairstyles. Clients preparing for special occasions such as weddings, graduations, birthdays, and parties. Women who prefer convenient salon services, including house-call appointments.

2 Website Goals and Objectives:
•	Website Goals:  The main goals of the Installations By Tsakani website are to increase brand awareness by introducing the salon to new customers and showcasing the quality of services offered. The website will also generate leads by encouraging visitors to contact the salon through WhatsApp, phone calls, or online enquiries. It will showcase the salon’s services and portfolio by displaying hair installation services, prices, and photos to build trust and attract potential clients. In addition, the website will provide valuable information such as hair care tips, salon policies, operating hours, services, and location to help customers make informed decisions.




•	Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): One of the main KPIs is Customer engagement and will be measured by the amount of time visitors spend on the website, the number of pages they view, and their interactions with the content. The salon will also monitor portfolio views to determine how many visitors explore the gallery and service pages. Customer reviews and ratings will be used to measure customer satisfaction and build trust with new clients. In addition, the website will track the number of visitors who follow the salon’s social media pages through the website, helping to increase its online presence. Website performance on mobile devices, including loading speed and ease of use, will also be monitored to ensure a positive user experience. 
3 Website Analysis: 
•	Strengths: The Installations By Tsakani website has a clean, attractive and professional design that represents the salon’s brand. It has simple navigation, informative content about the salon and its services, and is designed to work on both computers and mobile devices. Using HTML, CSS and JavaScript also make the website affordable because these technologies are free to use.
•	Weaknesses: One weakness is that the website may have limited interactive features, such customer reviews. The gallery may also have a limited number of hairstyle examples, making it difficult for customers to see the full range of services. The website also needs regular updates to ensure that prices, services, promotions and contact information remain accurate.
•	Areas for improvements: The website can be improved by adding an online booking system, customer reviews and a larger gallery showing different hairstyles and installations. A contact form and FAQ section could also improve communication with customers. Regular updates should be made to keep the website information accurate and ensure that customers have a better overall experience.

4 Proposed Website Features and Functionality:
 Homepage: The homepage should create a strong first impression by displaying the salon’s logo, a welcoming message, attractive images of hairstyles, featured services and clear call-to-action buttons such as “Contact Us.” It should also include easy navigation to all other pages.
About Us: This page should introduce Installations By Tsakani, sharing the salon’s story, mission, vision, values, and commitment to providing professional hair services. It should also highlight the salon’s experience, qualifications, and dedication to customer satisfaction to build trust with visitors.
Services Page: The services page should list all the services offered in the salon, including wig installations, treatments, styling, and maintenance. Where possible, the pricing should be in place for each service so customers can get the best service that is right for them.
Gallery: There should be high-quality photos of completed hairstyles and installations in a gallery for prospective clients to see and feel confident in the quality of services that they can get.
Contact Page: The contact page should provide the salon’s phone number, email address, physical location (Thulamahashe | West Acres), operating hours and an enquiry form. 


Desired Functionality:
•	The website will have a responsive design that works well on desktops, tablets, and smartphones. It will have easy navigation with a clear menu structure and provide contact information for customer enquiries. The website will include social media links for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp, as well as an image gallery displaying high-quality hairstyle photos. It will also be designed for fast loading speeds to provide a smooth user experience. WhatsApp integration will allow customers to communicate with the salon instantly, while simple content management will allow the salon owner to easily update services, prices, promotions, and gallery images.

6 Technical Requirements:
•	Hosting Requirements: 
The website hosting should provide 99.9% uptime to ensure that the website remains available to customers. It should offer fast loading speeds for a smooth browsing experience and at least 5–10 GB of storage for storing images and website files. The hosting should also include email hosting for professional email addresses, such as info@installationsbytsakani.co.za. In addition, 24/7 customer support would be beneficial for resolving technical issues quickly and keeping the website running efficiently.

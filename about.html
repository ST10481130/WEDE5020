ABOUT US

Providing the finest hair crowns

Our Story

Installations By Tsakani was created from a passion for beauty, creativity, and helping women feel confident in their own crowns. What started as a love for hairstyling grew into a salon dedicated to providing quality hair services and beautiful transformations. We believe that every client deserves to feel special, confident, and beautiful. From braiding and wig installations to styling and hair treatments, we put care and attention into every hairstyle we create. Our journey continues to grow through the support of our clients and our commitment to making every visit a memorable experience. At Installations By Tsakani, we don’t just do hair , we create confidence, one crown at a time.

Mission

At Installations By Tsakani, our mission is to provide high-quality hair services that enhance beauty, boost confidence, and celebrate individuality. We are committed to delivering exceptional care, creativity, and professionalism in every hairstyle we create, ensuring each client leaves feeling valued and beautiful.

Vision

Our vision is to become a leading and trusted hair salon known for excellence, innovation, and empowering women through beauty. We aim to build a space where every client experiences transformation, confidence, and a sense of pride in their crown.

Why Choose Us?

At Installations By Tsakani, we believe your hair is more than just a hairstyle, it is a reflection of your beauty and confidence. We are committed to providing quality hair services, personalised care, and beautiful results that make every client feel special. With creativity, professionalism, and attention to detail, we strive to give you a hairstyle that makes you feel confident and proud of your crown. Choose us because your beauty deserves the best.

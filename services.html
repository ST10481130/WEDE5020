OUR SERVICES

At Installations By Tsakani, we offer a range of quality hair services designed to enhance your beauty and confidence. Whether you’re looking for a fresh installation, a stylish new look, or a beautiful transformation, we are here to bring your vision to life. Let us create the perfect crown for you.

Our Pricelist

  • Hair Installations - R200

    A simple, neat installation of your wig, including securing and styling it into a natural-looking finish. Ideal for everyday wear when you want a quick and affordable installation.

  • Hair Styling - R300

    Includes the basic installation plus additional styling such as curls, waves, straightening, parting, or other finishing touches. Perfect when you want your wig installed and styled ready to wear.

  • Bridal Installation - R800

    A premium installation designed for brides and special occasions. This includes detailed preparation, secure installation, and professional styling to create an elegant, long-lasting look for your wedding day or special event.

  • Hair Revamp - R100

    Refreshes an existing wig or hairpiece to make it look and feel better again. Depending on the hair’s condition, this can include washing, conditioning, detangling, and basic restyling.

  • Lace Cleaning - R50

    A cleaning service specifically for the lace area of a wig. It removes old adhesive/glue and buildup from the lace so the wig can be reinstalled or maintained properly.
